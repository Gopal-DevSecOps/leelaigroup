About us

Leelai Group is part of Leelai Group, bringing manpower, education, skill development and industry solutions together.

Who we are

Leelai Group focuses on manpower and workforce solutions, supporting organizations with skilled and required manpower for various operational and technical requirements.

Our services include manpower support for electrical work and skilled service-engineer requirements covering inspection, health check-ups, troubleshooting, repair, installation and commissioning of electrical equipment such as motors, drives, panels and busbars.

Part of Leelai Group

We operate as part of Leelai Group, a diversified group with a stated focus across education, skill development, placements, construction, manufacturing, finance, healthcare and project services.
